# Example journey: a lesson on compound interest

The [designed lesson walkthrough](./lesson-walkthrough.html) follows one teacher from preparation to the end of class. It shows the materials in the imagined lesson package, how the teacher adapts it, and how the lesson flows.

This is an illustration of OLAM's vision, not a finished product. The example is specific so the process is easy to picture; the materials and capabilities described are imagined.

## The flow

1. **Prepare:** open one coordinated package with videos, a slide deck, visualizations, student handouts, curated links, and a teacher briefing.
2. **Adapt:** remove or change material that does not suit the class, add the teacher's own context, and keep advanced ideas optional.
3. **Teach:** the teacher leads a 45-minute class, using short visual explanations between discussion and practice.
4. **Continue:** offer optional practice or trusted resources for learners who want to go further.

The teacher stays in control throughout.
