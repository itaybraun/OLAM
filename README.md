# OLAM

**Open Learning Adaptive Materials**

An experimental project exploring how AI can change the way we teach and learn.

---

## What is OLAM?

Education is entering a new era. AI can now explain, adapt, translate, quiz, and iterate on learning content in ways that were impractical a few years ago. OLAM is a playground for finding out what works.

This is not a finished product or a polished curriculum. It is a working lab where we try ideas, keep what helps learners, and discard what doesn't.

## What we're exploring

- **Learning materials**: lessons, exercises, summaries, and study guides, created, refined, or personalized with AI
- **Methods**: approaches to teaching and learning that AI makes newly possible, such as adaptive pacing, tailored explanations, and instant feedback
- **Tools**: the apps, models, and workflows that help build and deliver better learning experiences
- **Processes**: how content moves from idea to draft to review to the learner, and where AI helps or gets in the way

## Principles

1. **Open**: materials and findings are shared so others can use, remix, and improve them.
2. **Adaptive**: learning should fit the learner, not the other way around.
3. **Experimental**: we try things, measure what happens, and stay honest about results.
4. **Human-centered**: AI supports teachers and learners. It doesn't replace judgment, care, or curiosity.
